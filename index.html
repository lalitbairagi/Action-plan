A specific viral-content initiative

100 Voices for Mahatma Phule

Invite 100 people to speak for 20 seconds about education, equality, social reform and cultural identity.

Ask every participant to publish or share their video through their own account.

This gives Kalapath an opportunity to enter new audience networks without paying each personality for a separate promotional advertisement.

11 Management rules

Three non-negotiable management rules

1

No vanity metrics.

A creator’s follower count is not a campaign result. Actual views, local audience concentration, registrations and attendance are what matter.

2

Every audience source must be measurable.

Use separate QR codes for colleges, organisations, creators and public registrations. Count actual campus entries separately from auditorium ticket scans.

3

Capacity and safety come before footfall.

The auditorium has a 2,400-person capacity. The outdoor campus requires its own approved occupancy and crowd-management plan. Never treat 25,000 cumulative visits as permission for 25,000 simultaneous visitors.

Final recommendation

My final recommendation

Appoint the campaign head immediately and give them authority over the digital, PR and ground mobilisation teams.

The first deliverable should be a live database containing:

  • 40 colleges and their coordinators.
  • 50 creators and their commercial terms.
  • 30 cultural and community organisations.
  • 10 media contacts.
  • Three artist promotional schedules.
  • Daily registration and viewership figures.

One reality check

3 crore views with this budget is already an ambitious target, and 5 crore requires unusually strong distribution by the artists and partner networks.

Secure those distribution commitments before presenting the target as a delivery promise.
